### Font rule by output format

`safe-stack` = name the design's safe-stack fallback fonts (the design's own font may not be installed on the reader's machine); `embed` = the design's own fonts may be embedded; `inline-webfont` = webfont in the HTML.

- docx: safe-stack
- html: inline-webfont
- pdf: embed
- png: embed
- pptx: embed
