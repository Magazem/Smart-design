## D.1 Method (reproducible)

1. **Title slide (E2-E4).** I took the first listed preview whose aspect ratio lies in 1.2-2.4.
   - NPM:049's preview 1 (`footer.png`, 1715x50) is not a slide, so preview 2 was coded. This is the case E2 anticipates, so no items-csv regeneration is needed.
   - Where the one listed image is a sheet of miniature slides (portrait stacks, master grids, slide-sorter strips, overlapping cards), the top-left (or topmost) miniature was coded as if it were the whole slide (E4). It was cropped with `--crop` for the pixel tests.
   - Slides that carry a body block were coded as `no-title-slide` (E3); their largest line is the title.
   - Transparent previews (all MS thumbnails, several LO/NPM PNGs) were composited on white before measuring.
2. **Heading (§1).**
   - **Step 1, declared title face**, read from the item's own source files. Fetched 2026-09-24; nothing was rendered.
     - npm: the latest tarball from `registry.npmjs.org` (2 requests: metadata + tarball). Read `package.json` `slidev.defaults.fonts` and the CSS/Vue `h1` / cover-title rules, resolving CSS variables.
     - LibreOffice: the extension download (2 requests: detail page + file). Read `styles.xml`, first `*-title` presentation style `style:font-name`, mapped to its `style:font-face` `svg:font-family`.
     - Microsoft: the `.pptx` found via the MS Create presentations page (thumbnail uuid → pptx link) and then downloaded. Title placeholder of the first slide (via `presentation.xml` relationships), then layout, then master `titleStyle`. All 25 inherit `+mj-lt`, i.e. the theme `majorFont`.
     - Family → class: Google Fonts `category` from `https://fonts.google.com/metadata/fonts` (fetched once, 1,946 families). Non-Google families by foundry class.
   - **Step 2, glyph test**, only where no single declared face exists (`glyph-judged` in the note): theme variants, a missing display-font definition, or an unknown non-Google class.
3. **Colour use (§2)**, title slide only, with two scripts:
   - `px2.py` builds a 20x20 grid colour-class background, extends it by the rule tolerances (achromatic ΔL ≤ 0.03; chromatic Δhue ≤ 15° and ΔL ≤ 0.05), and excludes photo/illustration boxes given per item. **Fill share** is the morphological opening (square side = 8% of slide height) of non-background pixels with L ≤ 0.90. **Hues** are 30° families of chromatic non-background pixels ≥ 0.2%; adjacent families are checked for the ≥ 30° test on the dominant sampled hues.
   - `a7.py` performs the A7(b) test: indigo (230-255°) and purple (265-295°) chromatic shares and their adjacency; PRESENT if both ≥ 1% and adjacency ≥ 0.2%. It also computes photo areas as the share of pixels differing from the ground inside a box.
   - Gradient grounds are treated as one background region (`--bg grad`).
4. **Title-slide layout (§3)**, measured in slide-width fractions: photo/illustration area, title-block horizontal extent and centre. Any value within 10% of a threshold is noted.
5. **Admissibility (§4 + §5).** A3 per C14 (running/bullet text or ≥ 3-line blocks directly on gradient/texture/photo); A5 per C20 (≥ 3 identical discrete motifs); A7 per §4 (sole-accent Office blue, or an indigo→purple gradient); A6 sampled where a title line sits on a photo or gradient.
