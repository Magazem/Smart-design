## RM.2 What could be seen: C13 applied to a cover-only preview (disclosed limitation)
- The catalogue publishes **one 400×519 webp thumbnail per template, and it is the cover** (page 1) for all 10. No other size exists: the `/thumbnails/800/` and `/thumbnails/1024/` paths return 404. The Word sitemap has no per-template detail pages. The other link is the Office Online viewer (JS, not static).
- C13: header treatment, heading class and colour use are coded **from the cover**, which was seen for all 10.
- C13 also asks for columns, body class, rules/boxes and density **from the first running-text page**. That page is **not published as an image**, and templates are never rendered (§3.3). Resolution, stated here and applied to all 10:
  - **body class** is taken from the **declared** font (§4: "When a declared font is visible in source … theme file, use it"). The .docx was downloaded to the system temp dir (never into the repo) and read as XML, not rendered. None of the 10 sets a font on the `Normal` style, so body text uses the theme **minor** font. The major/minor pairs are listed per item.
  - **columns** is taken from the declared `<w:cols>` of every section: all 10 declare 1 column in every section. Coded `1`, with the caveat that a layout table or text box on an unseen page could still form columns. `txbxContent` and `<w:tbl>` counts were read only for the segments the XML marks as running text.
  - **rules/boxes** and **density** are coded **`unknown`**. They cannot be read from source without laying the page out. Filling uses the family default for these variants (§7 last sentence, the same treatment as 82a C7 for decks).
  - The C13 running-page number is recorded as "not seen". The XML probe (`w:lastRenderedPageBreak` segments) finds a ≥250-word segment in only 3 of 10 templates (MSR:002, 003, 008). The other 7 carry short placeholder text, so a "first page with ≥250 words" may not exist in them at all.
- **Cover page (variant) = yes** for all 10: each thumbnail is a cover (title, author/date, image or colour field, no running text).
- **82a C14** (cover text on photos): no cover title of 3+ lines sits directly on a photo. Display text on photo (MSR:003 "20XX", MSR:009 by-line strip) is evidently high-contrast. All ratios below are `lib/color.contrast_ratio` on thumbnail samples, so they are estimates (C9).

## RM.6 Bias statement
Microsoft Create is Microsoft's editorial selection, the first static slice of one Word category (page order is not a metric). Half the items are **student** reports and papers, not business reports, so the population mixes school and corporate genres. All are Word-authored with Office theme fonts, which biases body class toward bundled fonts (Calibri, Georgia, Franklin Gothic, Gill Sans) and colour toward theme accent fills. Only covers were seen, so the body-page features rest on declared source (fonts, columns) or are unknown. Compare ARC (`report-corpus-l3.md`), where header was image-hero 14/14: here the cover headers are more varied. Prevalence of curation, not usage; popularity is not quality (§13).

## RM.8 Ambiguities met (for 82a)
1. **C13 without a running-text preview.** The vendor publishes covers only. Using declared fonts and columns and coding rules/density `unknown` is this coder's reading and needs ratifying or overruling. The alternative, opening the Office Online viewer, would be a rendered view and is not used.
2. "First page with ≥250 words" may not exist in a placeholder template (7/10 have no ≥250-word segment in the XML).
3. Full-page colour grounds on covers (MSR:001, 005, 007) are coded fill-blocks, and header text on them is coded band (C17/C18 read literally). A reviewer could argue that a full-page ground is "background", as the ARC coder did for GRAWE (R.5 item 2). §4 excludes only "photos and the deck background".
4. Photos whose top edge sits at 20-27% of page height (MSR:001, 005, 008) are not image-hero under the literal C1 test.
