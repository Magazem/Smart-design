(48 items, preview URLs only). Procedures: `research/82a-general.md` §A (header treatment) and
§B (colour use), read together with `research/82a-clarifications-1..4.md` and, where 82a-general
explicitly inherits its procedure/thresholds, `research/82a-cv.md` §"Decision procedure".
Did NOT open `invoice-corpus.md`, `invoice-second-coder.md` or `invoice-agreement.md`.

`fetch.py` (PDF pages rendered via PyMuPDF at 2x, GIF first frame, WEBP/PNG/JPG loaded directly).

which is used directly for any WCAG contrast check needed). All coordinates below are pixel
coordinates in the downloaded preview at its native resolution (each item's `size=WxH` is reported
where measured).
