## 2. Method
Same as `letter-second-coder.md` §2. Cover-letter specifics:
- 82a-general §A.1: title = the applicant's name; header block = name + role + contact lines; boundary = date, recipient block, reference/subject line or salutation. Lines belonging to the boundary element (e.g. a subject-line underline) never count.
- Photo variant (§4). Letterhead position by 82a C19 (ratified for letter and cover-letter).
- Columns by 82a C22/C28: sender, contact and recipient/address columns never make a column, so C1 is judged on the body only.
- Admissibility: §5 A1-A8 plus the cover-letter fail constraints C1-C6.
- PDF previews (GH:021, 060, 066, 079, 122) are the repos' published PDFs; page 1 was rendered with PyMuPDF at 90 dpi and embedded font names were read.

## 3. Exposure disclosure (82a C12) and sensitivity run
