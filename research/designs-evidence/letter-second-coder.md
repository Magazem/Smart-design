## 2. Method
- Previews: each csv `preview_url`, downloaded to `tmp-sc3/` (deleted at the end). PDF previews (GH:009, 030, 032, 039, 051) are the repos' own published example PDFs; page 1 was rendered with PyMuPDF at 90 dpi and embedded font names were read. The transparent LibreOffice PNG (LOL:045) was composited on white. LOL:046's preview is the 233×300 listing image (low confidence, 82a-cv A3).
- **Header treatment:** 82a-general §A. Letter title = sender name (first line of the sender block); letterhead = sender name + address/contact/slogan; boundary = date, recipient block, reference line or salutation. Logos, crests and wordmark lock-ups are never the title and never make split. `align=right` is recorded in the note.
- **Colour use:** 82a-general §B, measured with a PIL script (B1a modal background on a 20×20 grid; B3 fill blocks by morphological opening with a square of side 5% of the short side; B4 chromatic S ≥ 0.20, 0.12 ≤ L ≤ 0.90; B5/B6 presence and clustering). Core stroke colours were sampled where a call was close.
- **Other features:** research/82 §4. Letterhead position by 82a C19. Columns by 82a C22/C28 (address/routing blocks never make a column). Density by line count normalised to A4/Letter. Admissibility by §5 A1-A8 (letter has no family fail constraint).
