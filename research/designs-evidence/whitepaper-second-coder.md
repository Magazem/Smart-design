## 3. Method
- Previews: each csv `preview_url` (8 published example PDFs from the repos, the Overleaf template PDF, one LibreOffice screenshot), downloaded to the system temp dir only. PDF pages rendered with PyMuPDF at 70 dpi; words per page and embedded fonts read from the PDF.
- **C13:** header treatment, heading class and colour use from the **cover**; columns, body class, rules/boxes and density from the **first running-text page** (first page with ≥250 words). Where a template has **no cover** (page 1 opens the text), page 1 is used for everything and cover = `no`. Where **no page reaches 250 words** (placeholder templates), the page with the most running text after the cover is used, as stated per item.
- **Header/colour:** 82a-general §A/§B (report/whitepaper: title = largest text on the cover; header block = title + contiguous subtitle/author/organisation/date lines; no boundary). Logos/crests/mascots excluded; illustrations excluded (B1b); data charts are marks (C30). A box edge spanning ≥80% of the page counts as a line; section-heading rules never count (82a-cv A2).
- **C14:** no cover title in this pool sits on a photo, so there is nothing to sample for contrast. **C29:** thresholds are applied literally at the boundary.
- **Admissibility:** §5 A1-A8 (whitepaper has no family fail constraint). No exclusions.
