# Poster — header treatment + colour use recode under research/82a-general.md

Recoder: fresh worker, has never coded the poster family before (no corpus/agreement/second-coder
files for poster were opened; item identity comes ONLY from `research/designs-evidence/poster-items-github.csv`,
40 rows). Scope: `header treatment` and `colour use` only, per `research/82a-general.md` §A/§B and
the clarification files (82a-clarifications-1..5.md), especially the poster/flyer/infographic/one-pager
row of §A.1 (title = largest text anywhere on page 1, NOT restricted to the top 20%; header block =
title + contiguous subtitle/date/venue; boundary = first following text block) and C1 (image-hero
literal test: an image/illustration intersecting the top 20% of the page AND its own area ≥30% of
the PAGE area; a university logo/crest is never the title and never counts toward image-hero).

**Method.** Previews were downloaded with a browser User-Agent to
`research/designs-evidence/tmp-rprc/poster/` (deleted at the end of this task). Raster previews
(png/jpg, with any `?raw=true` / `%3Fraw%3Dtrue` URL suffix stripped before fetch) were converted to
PNG; `.pdf` previews were rendered at page 1 with PyMuPDF (`fitz.Matrix(2,2)`), then the longest side
was capped at 2500px with PIL LANCZOS resize (posters are large A0 sheets; this keeps files
manageable — resolutions actually measured are reported per item). `GH:014`'s `preview_url` is a
`reporoster.com/stars/...` GitHub star-history badge, not a design preview at all — uncodeable per
C2, excluded from N. `GH:002`'s literal `%3Fraw%3Dtrue`-suffixed URL 404'd; stripping the decoded
`?raw=true` suffix (and then the bare URL, which also works) fetched it successfully.

Scripts `tmp-rprc/poster/analyze.py` (20×20 background-grid sampler, region-median sampler, point
sampler, HSL via `colorsys`) and `tmp-rprc/poster/huescan.py` (stride-3 full-image hue scan,
bucketing `S>=0.20 & 0.12<=L<=0.90` pixels into 10°-wide hue bins with area-share, plus the same
background candidates) are adapted line-for-line from `tmp-invrc/analyze.py` / `tmp-invrc/huescan.py`
(a different family's leftover scratch, read as a working reference). Every numeric percentage or
hex below is copy-pasted from real script output on the saved PNG, never eyeballed; header-treatment
band/rule/split geometry (page-width %, band-height %, title-centre offset) is read visually against
the image's known pixel dimensions and cross-checked with `region_median`/`point` where the call is
close to a threshold — such cases are marked **low-confidence** below. Page scope = the whole poster
sheet (page 1 of the render), per §A.1's poster row.

**Colour-clustering shortcut (disclosed).** For §B4–B6, this recoder uses `huescan.py`'s 10°-bucketed
chromatic area-shares (S≥0.20, 0.12≤L≤0.90) as a direct proxy for "counted elements": adjacent bins
within 30° are merged into one cluster (B6); a cluster is treated as clearing B5 presence if its
merged area share is **≥0.5% of the sampled pixels** (this is the same bar B5 sets for "one element
covering ≥0.5% of page area", and on a poster a hue that shows up at all in a stride-3 scan across a
whole A0 sheet is essentially always contributed by ≥2 separate glyphs/rules rather than one hairline
hyperlink); bins that stay under 0.5% after merging are treated as anti-alias fringe / noise (as in
82a-general's own GH:030 worked example) and dropped. B3 (fill-blocks) is always decided first, from
the background-grid's 2nd/3rd-ranked colour classes (a genuine block shows as a large, non-thin,
non-background colour share; thin rules/text scatter across many small hue bins instead). University
logos/crests are excluded wholesale (B1b) including their internal palette; where a small hue cluster
plausibly belongs only to an excluded logo, this is disclosed and the cluster is dropped.
