## 2. Method
- Previews: the csv `preview_url`, downloaded to `tmp-sc6/` (deleted at the end). GH:075's preview is the repo's published PDF; page 1 was rendered with PyMuPDF (90 dpi) and its embedded fonts read (Calibri). Screenshots with browser chrome or canvases are coded on the document sheet only (82a-cv A3).
- **Header treatment:** 82a-general §A. Invoice title = largest text in the top 30% (issuer name or "INVOICE"; logos and logo placeholders never count); header block = title + issuer identity + document meta above the boundary; boundary = first recipient block or line-item table. Table borders and header rules belong to the boundary and never count. A box edge counts as a line when the box spans ≥80% of the page width.
- **Colour use:** 82a-general §B, measured with a PIL script (modal background on a 20×20 grid; fill blocks by morphological opening with a square of side 5% of the short side; chromatic S ≥ 0.20, 0.12 ≤ L ≤ 0.90; B5 presence; B6 clustering). UI artefacts (buttons, field shading, Excel filter arrows) are excluded (B1d).
- **Variants:** totals position {right-bottom, full-width-bottom, other} and table rules {none, header-and-total, row-hairlines, all-cells} (§4). `unknown` is used where the preview does not show the element (MS:002 is a crop without totals).
- **Admissibility:** §5 A1-A8 + C7 (table rules = all-cells → fail). Contrast by `lib/color.contrast_ratio` on sampled hexes (C9 estimates).
